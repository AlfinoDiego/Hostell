How Hostell Formed

Hostell Area has a unique origin story that began on February 7, 2024, when two founders - Chaelino & 2Pac - came together with the original owner Cece. The name "Hostell" was inspired by a popular manga, and thus Hostell Fams was born.

"We started with just a vision and brotherhood. From two founders and an owner to a family of hundreds."

From its inception on February 7, 2024, Hostell Fams grew rapidly, reaching an impressive 700 active members on WhatsApp. The community was vibrant and active, creating a strong sense of belonging among its members.

However, by mid-to-late 2024, the community went through a period of hiatus (vakum) as activities slowed down. This quiet period was followed by a rebirth in December 2024, when the community was revived as Hostell Reborn.

The rebirth was short-lived due to internal conflicts that emerged within the community. Faced with these challenges and an overwhelming number of people wanting to join, the difficult decision was made to temporarily deactivate the community to regroup and restructure.

On November 26, 2025, Hostell made its triumphant return, now rebranded as Hostell Area under new leadership. The community is currently owned and guided by Pabloo, who is steering Hostell Area toward a new chapter of growth, unity, and street culture excellence.

Today, Hostell Area stands as more than just a crew – it's a phoenix that rose from its ashes, stronger and more determined. We've learned from our past, evolved through our challenges, and emerged with a clearer vision: to create a space where real ones can connect, grow, and represent authentic street culture without compromise.
